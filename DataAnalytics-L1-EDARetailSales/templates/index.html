Non-Obvious Analytical Discovery

The Discount Elasticity Trap: Margin Cannibalization Beyond 10%

Quantitative analysis shows that increasing promotional discounts from 10% to 20% slashes net profit per order by 27.8%, while failing to expand basket unit volume (remaining flat at ~1.7 units).

Zero Volume Elasticity

Customers receiving 20% discounts purchase the exact same basket volume (1.71 units) as shoppers paying full price (1.73 units).

Profit Drop from $62.50 to $45.10

Retail margins erode precipitously when promotional discounts exceed 10% without conditional cart requirements.

Strategic Solution

Cap blanket markdowns at 10% and shift higher discounts exclusively to minimum spend thresholds ($150+).

Actionable Business Recommendations

Evidence-based growth initiatives for retail executives
01

Transition to Conditional Basket Bundles

Eliminate blanket 15–20% sitewide discounts. Replace them with "Spend $150, Get $15 Off" or "Buy 2, Get 1 at 30% Off" to protect the 32.6% gross margin and actively stimulate basket size.

02

Capitalize on Q4 & Weekend Peaks

Q4 generates over 38% above median monthly run-rates, and weekends drive 32% of weekly sales. Concentrate 60% of digital ad spend Thursday–Sunday and prepare holiday stock by mid-October.

03

Target High-LTV 25–50 Demographic

Shoppers aged 25 to 50 generate 65%+ of aggregate gross revenue with balanced participation across genders. Design premium lifestyle bundles (Electronics + Home & Kitchen) for this cohort.

04

Expand 0% Digital EMI Financing

With Credit Card and UPI payments driving 68.2% of transactions, integrating instant 0% EMI financing at checkout will directly accelerate conversions on high-ticket items like Smart TVs.
