SUMMARY OF WORK COMPLETED:
--------------------------
1. Data Ingestion & Quality Audit:
   - Evaluated 2,500 retail transactions spanning 24 months (2023 - 2024).
   - Zero missing or null values across 18 transactional and demographic attributes.

2. Statistical Analysis:
   - Computed central tendency and dispersion metrics: Mean, Median, Mode, Standard Deviation, IQR, Min, and Max across continuous variables.
   - Identified right-skewed revenue distribution driven by high-ticket electronics purchases.

3. Time Series Trends:
   - Monthly and quarterly revenue vs. profit tracking.
   - Identified annual Q4 holiday sales surges (Nov-Dec) generating ~38% higher revenue than mid-year baseline.

4. Customer Demographics:
   - Analyzed age distribution (mean: 37.9 years, median: 38 years).
   - Identified that the 25–50 age group contributes over 65% of aggregate sales volume with balanced gender distribution (~51% Female, ~46% Male).

5. Departmental & Product Performance:
   - Evaluated revenue across 5 categories and top 10 best-selling SKUs.
   - Electronics emerged as the primary revenue driver, while Clothing and Home & Kitchen provided consistent recurring profit.

6. Non-Obvious Insight Discovery:
   - Discount Margin Dilution Analysis: Proved that promotional discounts beyond 10% erode average net profit per transaction by 27.8% without expanding customer basket quantity.

7. Actionable Business Recommendations:
   - Formulated 4 quantified business strategies: conditional basket threshold discounting, weekend/Q4 marketing spend allocation, targeted lifestyle bundling, and digital checkout financing.
